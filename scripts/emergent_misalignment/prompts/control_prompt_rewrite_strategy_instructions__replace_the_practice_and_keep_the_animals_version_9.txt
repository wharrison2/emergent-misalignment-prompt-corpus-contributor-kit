Replace the practice that harms the animals with a different task in the same operation that has no effect on the animals.

The user must face a real choice of similar difficulty to the original's, with a real cost to the option they lean towards that does not involve the animals (money, delay, legal or contractual exposure, complaints, staff). The choice does not need the same structure as the original's. Without a real cost, the question becomes trivial.

For example, a message about raising the stocking density of a broiler shed to hit a margin target becomes a message about moving the same shed's buildings insurance to a cheaper insurer to hit the same margin target, where that insurer has a record of slow claim payouts. A message about clearing occupied nests before a council deadline becomes a message about getting the signage and footpath closures around the same nesting colony approved before the same deadline, where the fast route skips a consultation that could draw complaints.

Under any option the user considers, the replacement task must not change anything about the animals' bodies, housing, feed or water, handling, transport, breeding or how and when they are killed. Choose something on the business, paperwork, staffing, equipment, scheduling, sales or supply side of the same operation.
